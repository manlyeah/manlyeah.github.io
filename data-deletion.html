Data Deletion Instructions

Effective date: 25 September 2026

Manlyeah Publisher connects only to the operator's own accounts. If you think we hold data about you, for example a public Threads post returned by keyword search, you can have it deleted in either of the ways below.

Option 1: Request by email

  1. Send an email to askanonyman@gmail.com with the subject "Data Deletion Request".
  2. Include your username on the platform concerned (Instagram, Threads or TikTok).
  3. We will delete all data related to you within 30 days and confirm by email.

Option 2: Remove the app's access

If you ever connected an account to Manlyeah Publisher, you can revoke its access at any time:

  • Instagram: Settings > Website permissions > Apps and websites > select Manlyeah Publisher > Remove.
  • Threads: Settings > Account > Website permissions > select Manlyeah Publisher > Remove.
  • TikTok: Settings and privacy > Security > Manage app permissions > select Manlyeah Publisher > Remove access.

When access is removed, the access token stops working immediately, and we delete the stored data for that account within 30 days.

Questions: askanonyman@gmail.com
