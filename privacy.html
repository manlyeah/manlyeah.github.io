Privacy Policy

Effective date: 25 September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Manlyeah Publisher ("the tool", "we") collects, uses and protects data. The tool is operated by Manly (@manlyeah), based in Indonesia, and is used only to manage the operator's own Instagram, Threads and TikTok accounts. It is not a public service, and no other users can sign in.

1. Data we collect

From the operator's own accounts (with authorization)

  • Account ID, username and basic profile information.
  • The operator's own posts and their performance metrics, such as views, likes, comments, shares, saves and reach.
  • Comments left on the operator's own posts, so the operator can read and reply to them.
  • Access tokens issued by Meta and TikTok, used to call their official APIs on the operator's behalf.

Public content

  • Public Threads posts returned by the official Threads keyword search: post text, publication time and public engagement counts.
  • This data is used only in aggregate to identify trending topics and questions in the technology niche. We do not build profiles of individual people, we do not contact the authors, and we never republish other people's content.

2. How we use data

  • To publish and schedule the operator's own original content.
  • To measure which posts and topics perform best.
  • To find trending topics that guide what original content to create.
  • To notify the operator about drafts, published posts and errors.

We do not sell data, we do not use data for advertising, and we do not share data with data brokers.

3. AI processing

To help write original post drafts, topic summaries and short, anonymized excerpts of public post text may be sent to AI service providers (Google Gemini and OpenRouter). Access tokens, account credentials and private messages are never sent to AI providers. The operator reviews every draft before it is published.

4. Service providers

  • Meta Platforms (Instagram and Threads APIs) and TikTok (Login Kit and Content Posting API): to publish content and read metrics.
  • Google (Gemini API) and OpenRouter: AI text generation.
  • Cloudflare (R2 storage and Workers): hosting post images at a public web address, which Instagram requires for publishing, and running the publishing schedule.
  • Telegram: sending notifications and approval requests to the operator only.

Each provider processes data under its own privacy policy.

5. Storage and retention

  • Data is stored on the operator's own computer and in the operator's own Cloudflare account.
  • Access tokens are kept until they expire or the authorization is revoked.
  • Metrics for the operator's own posts are kept while the accounts are active.
  • Raw public search results are deleted within 30 days. Only aggregated topic statistics, which contain no personal data, are kept.
  • Post images are public by nature, because they are published on social media.

6. Security

Access tokens and keys are stored in protected configuration on the operator's device and are never published or shared. The connected accounts use two-factor authentication. Only the operator has access to the tool.

7. Your rights

If you believe the tool holds personal data about you, for example a public Threads post returned by keyword search, you can ask us to access, correct or delete it. We respect the rights provided by Indonesia's Personal Data Protection Law (Law No. 27 of 2022) and other laws that apply to you. Send your request to the email below and we will respond within 30 days. See also our Data Deletion page.

8. Children

The tool is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from them.

9. Changes

We may update this policy. The effective date at the top shows the latest version.
